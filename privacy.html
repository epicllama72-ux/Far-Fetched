FAR-FETCHED

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Far-Fetched is a free daily news game at farfetchedgame.com. This page explains what information the game keeps, why, and how to have it removed. Questions: playfarfetched@gmail.com.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your streak, past scores, wardrobe, passport stamps and game in progress are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device. You can delete them any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

WHAT IS SENT WHEN YOU PLAY

  • Scores for leaderboards. When you finish a game, we save a random player ID, your display name (one is made up for you unless you pick your own), your score, your answer to each headline and where you put each map pin. This powers the leaderboards, group boards and the "how everyone else did" stats. Other players can see your display name, score and pins.
  • Invites. If you arrive through a friend's share or group link, we save a short code for the player who invited you, so the game can count their invites.
  • Groups. If you create or join a group, we save the group's name and code and which player IDs belong to it.
  • Letters to the editor. If you send the feedback form, we save your answer with your random player ID, the day and your score, but no name.
  • Visit counts. We use GoatCounter to count page visits. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites.

SIGNING IN WITH GOOGLE (OPTIONAL)

You can play without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID with us. We use them only to sign you in and keep your progress (streak, scores, display name, groups, wardrobe, passport, back issues and game in progress) saved to your account so it follows you to other devices. We never see your Google password, and we don't post anything to your Google account. Your email address is never shown to other players.

WHO HANDLES THE DATA

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages. Scores, groups, feedback, accounts and saved progress are stored with Supabase, which runs our database and sign-in. Sign-in is provided by Google. The sign-in code is loaded from jsDelivr, a public file server that receives none of your game data. We don't sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

DELETING YOUR DATA

Email playfarfetched@gmail.com and we will delete your account, saved progress and scores. If you signed in with Google, send it from that address so we can find your account. You can also remove Far-Fetched's access in your Google account settings at any time.

CHILDREN

Far-Fetched is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has signed in, email us and we will delete their account.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
