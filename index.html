        About Far-Fetched

        Far-Fetched is a free daily news game. In the Daily Dig you get seven weird headlines each day: five really happened and two were made up. Call each one real or fake, then pin the real ones on the map. Every point drops the digger's jaw deeper. The Daily Dig is the only mode that earns Wardrobe items and builds your Dig Kit. Fill the Blank and Politics are daily too, and Practice lets you play older stories without touching your streak.
