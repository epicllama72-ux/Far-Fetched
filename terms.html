Terms and Refunds

Last updated November 26, 2026

Far-Fetched is free to play. The Press Pass is an optional one-time purchase that adds extras. This page says what you get, how payment works and how to get your money back. Questions: playfarfetched@gmail.com.

WHAT YOU GET

  • Press Pass, $5: Unlimited rounds of every story in the archive, every back issue (free players get the last 5), and no ads, ever.
  • Press Pass + Jaw Drill, $10: everything in the Press Pass plus the strap-on jaw drill for your wardrobe. If you already have the pass, a second $5 payment adds the drill. The drill is cosmetic only: it does not change your score, depth or rank.

It is a one-time payment, not a subscription. Nothing renews.

HOW LONG IT LASTS

The pass belongs to the Google account you were signed in with when you paid, and it works on any device where you sign in with that account. It lasts for as long as Far-Fetched is online. If the game is ever closed down, we will say so on the site first. We may add things to the Press Pass over time, and we will not take away what you paid for.

NO ADS

Far-Fetched has no ads today. If we ever add any, Press Pass holders will not see them.

PAYMENT

Payment is handled by Stripe on its own secure page. We never see your card details. Stripe acts as the seller of your purchase, sends your receipt, and adds sales tax or VAT at checkout where it applies, so the total can be a little more than $5 or $10 in some places.

REFUNDS

You can have a full refund within 14 days of paying, for any reason. Email playfarfetched@gmail.com from the address on your receipt and say you would like a refund. We refund to your original payment method, and your bank may take a few days to show it.

Once a purchase is refunded, the Press Pass (and the jaw drill, if it was part of that purchase) is removed from your account. Your scores, streak and everything else are untouched.

After 14 days, write to us anyway if something went wrong, such as a payment that did not unlock your pass. We will fix it or refund it. If you live somewhere that gives you stronger consumer rights, those still apply.

IT DID NOT UNLOCK

Sign in with the same Google account you paid with, open the Press Pass page and tap Check again. It can take a minute to show. If it still does not, email us your receipt and we will sort it out.

CHANGES

If these terms change, we will update this page and the date at the top. Changes do not reduce what you have already paid for.
